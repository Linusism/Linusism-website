Creed























I pledge to Linus Torvalds as an incarnation of god


And Judd Vinet as the man who suffered

for our closed-source sins


Who created Arch and subjected himself

to the suffering that it is


And I applaud the torment every Arch user has to go through

to serve under Linus Torvalds


I believe that every Windows user should either switch to linux

on all compatible devices by any means necessary


Or be subject to the torture of the eternal Windows
