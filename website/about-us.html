Our beliefs
We worship Linus torvalds as an incarnation of the "god" (or personification) of the open source philosophy. The role of "god" is passed on with the most significant project in the open source community. You are not allowed to use Windows under any circumstances (on your personal computer). Avoid using it on other computers too at a reasonable cost. Other programs affiliated with Microsoft should also be avoided if no major sacrifice is involved, as should other closed-source programs with a functioning alternative
